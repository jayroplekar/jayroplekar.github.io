Confessions of an Almost Luddite

Notes from an engineer who keeps finding the reductionist explanation — and keeps being slightly wrong about what it means.

I am a recovering simulation engineer. I like problems reduced to their bare essence. I have a reliable habit of encountering new technology, correctly identifying its mechanism, and then underestimating what the mechanism produces. This series is a record of that habit.

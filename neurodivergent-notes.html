Notes from the Other Hand

On neurodivergence, and what happens when you stop trying to hold the pencil correctly.

I was told I was doing things wrong before I understood I was doing them differently. Left-handedness is a useful frame for this: same task, same goal, different wiring, and a world that spent centuries treating that difference as a defect. This series is an attempt to think clearly about neurodivergence — through that analogy, and through my own experience of it.
